How to Play
  1. Everyone answers the same question on their own sheet and passes the sheet on: nobody sees what was written before
  2. Eight questions: who, with whom, when, where and others. They come in sets (classic, fairy tale, detective story and four more), and every round draws its own
  3. At the end everyone reads out the story that came to them. The stories of every round played stay in the room to read again
